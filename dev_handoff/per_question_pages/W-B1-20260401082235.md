## Development Markers (3 specific + 1 generic 4th)

### 1. Positive effects of tourism

- **Score 1 (mentioned):** names a positive effect with no detail (e.g., tourism brings jobs)
- **Score 2 (developed):** names a positive effect AND explains who benefits or how (e.g., tourism brings jobs to hotels and restaurants)
- **Score 3 (extended):** all of 2 AND a named place, sector, or number (e.g., in my city thousands work in tourism — hotels, restaurants and tour guides)

### 2. Negative effects of tourism

- **Score 1 (mentioned):** names a negative effect with no detail (e.g., there is pollution)
- **Score 2 (developed):** names a negative effect AND explains the impact (e.g., crowds make daily life harder for locals)
- **Score 3 (extended):** all of 2 AND a named place, situation, or example (e.g., in Venice the canals are overcrowded and rents have doubled)

### 3. Impact on local people, businesses, and environment

- **Score 1 (mentioned):** mentions only one of [people / businesses / environment]
- **Score 2 (developed):** covers at least two of the three AND describes a specific impact for each
- **Score 3 (extended):** covers all three AND each with a specific impact, named place, or example

### 4. Additional on-topic development

- **Score 1 (mentioned):** introduces a DISTINCT extra on-topic point — not a rephrasing or repeat of any other requirement — within the question's subject, with no further detail
- **Score 2 (developed):** introduces a distinct extra on-topic point AND develops it meaningfully (a real reason, cause, or effect — not just length)
- **Score 3 (extended):** introduces a distinct extra on-topic point AND develops it AND supports it with a concrete specific that genuinely advances the argument
