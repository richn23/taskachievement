## Development Markers (3 specific + 1 generic 4th)

### 1. How shopping habits have changed over time

- **Score 1 (mentioned):** mentions a change without comparing past and present (e.g., people shop online)
- **Score 2 (developed):** clearly compares past and present (e.g., before people went to the high street, now most order on apps)
- **Score 3 (extended):** all of 2 AND a specific behaviour, year, or platform (e.g., in 2020 my mother started using Amazon for groceries; she used to walk to the corner shop)

### 2. Benefits of online shopping

- **Score 1 (mentioned):** names a benefit with no detail (e.g., it is convenient)
- **Score 2 (developed):** names a benefit AND explains who or why it helps (e.g., online shopping helps elderly people who cannot easily leave home)
- **Score 3 (extended):** all of 2 AND a specific person, situation, or example (e.g., my grandmother orders her medicine online and it arrives the same day)

### 3. Drawbacks of online shopping

- **Score 1 (mentioned):** names a drawback with no detail (e.g., it is risky)
- **Score 2 (developed):** names a drawback AND explains the impact (e.g., scams cost shoppers money because there is no shop to return to)
- **Score 3 (extended):** all of 2 AND a specific case, number, or example (e.g., last year I paid 30 euros for shoes that never arrived)

### 4. Additional on-topic development

- **Score 1 (mentioned):** introduces a DISTINCT extra on-topic point — not a rephrasing or repeat of any other requirement — within the question's subject, with no further detail
- **Score 2 (developed):** introduces a distinct extra on-topic point AND develops it meaningfully (a real reason, cause, or effect — not just length)
- **Score 3 (extended):** introduces a distinct extra on-topic point AND develops it AND supports it with a concrete specific that genuinely advances the argument
