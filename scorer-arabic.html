Scoring System

Six dimensions, each producing a 0–100 score. Some dimensions combine multiple sub-metrics. Arabic-specific metrics use either Arabic-aware LLM prompts or deterministic code over Arabic lexicons.

1 · Task Achievement

Did the response complete the task, meet length, and communicate the message?

WCC — Word Count Compliance

Scored 0–100 by comparing word count to the midpoint of the min/max range.

TA — Task Requirements

Each requirement marked PASS / FAIL using a strict test: response must name the topic AND give at least one specific detail.

Score = (PASS count + bonus) / (total + 1) × 100. Bonus for one extra developed on-topic point.

IE — Intelligibility & Effect

Scored 0–100 against a banded scale:

0–20 unintelligible · 21–40 barely intelligible · 41–60 partial · 61–75 mostly clear · 76–90 clear and effective · 91–100 fluent and compelling

2 · Content Quality

How well are ideas developed, supported, and extended?

CQ — Content & Ideas Markers

Each requirement scored 0–3:

0 absent · 1 mentioned · 2 developed (with reason / cause) · 3 extended (reason AND specific example)

Score = sum / max possible × 100.

3 · Style & Register (Arabic-specific)

Is the writing in formal Modern Standard Arabic (MSA) appropriate for the task?

MSA Consistency

Holistic 0–100. Does the writing read as composed MSA or as transcribed speech / dialect?

Dialect Influence Detection

LLM-only severity classification. Detects markers from Gulf / Egyptian / Levantine / Maghrebi dialects. Severity → score:

none → 100 · isolated → 90 · moderate → 70 · pervasive → 40

Combined Style & Register = MSA × 0.70 + Dialect × 0.30

4 · Language Range

How varied, flexible, and sophisticated is the language?

Vocabulary Diversity

Holistic 0–100. Range of distinct lexical items used (not penalised for derived forms of the same root).

Morphological Variety

Holistic 0–100. Evidence of masdar, active / passive participles, broken plurals, derived verb patterns.

Arabic Sentence Complexity

Holistic 0–100. Relative clauses, conditional sentences, nominal vs verbal sentence variety.

Combined Language Range = Vocab × 0.334 + Morphology × 0.333 + Sentence × 0.333

5 · Organisation & Coherence (Arabic-specific · code-only)

Is the writing structured logically with appropriate connectives?

Arabic Cohesive Devices

Deterministic scan of ~40 Arabic connectives across 6 categories: sequencing, addition, contrast, cause / effect, example, conclusion.

Score rewards variety and category spread, NOT raw frequency (to prevent mechanical stuffing).

Score = (categories used / 6) × 60% + (distinct types / 10) × 40%

6 · Language Accuracy

Is the language controlled and accurate enough for clear communication?

Grammar & Language Control

Holistic 0–100. Measures COMMUNICATIVE control, NOT native-speaker perfection.

Focus on: sentence formation, agreement, verb forms and tense, grammatical consistency, whether errors interfere with understanding.

Does NOT penalise: missing case endings (إعراب), minor spelling variation, stylistic preference.

Banded scale:

0–20 errors block understanding · 21–40 frequent basic errors · 41–60 noticeable but understandable · 61–80 good control · 81–100 strong consistent control

Summary

V1 = 6 dimensions, 11 metrics total (9 LLM, 2 code-based). Dialect Influence Detection was simplified from the spec's hybrid design to LLM-only for V1.

All metrics return 0–100. Each is reported separately with justification — there is no single Total Score in V1. Stakeholders read the dimension-by-dimension breakdown.

Arabic-specific metrics (MSA, Dialect, Cohesive Devices) use Arabic lexicons and Arabic-aware prompts. Other metrics reuse English scoring patterns with Arabic content as input.
